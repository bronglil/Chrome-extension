Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

SnapShot Studio is private by default. Everything it does runs locally in your browser. There is no backend server, no analytics, and no tracking. The extension does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

What data the extension handles

  • Captures, recordings, and documents are processed entirely on your device — held only in browser memory and in chrome.storage.local for the brief moment needed to hand a capture to the editor, then removed.
  • OCR and QR decoding run fully offline with bundled libraries. Image contents are never uploaded.
  • PDF signing happens on your device; your document is never sent anywhere.
  • Preferences, if stored, stay in chrome.storage.local on your device only.

Actions that leave your device (only when you ask)

  • Save to Downloads — writes a file to your own computer.
  • Copy to clipboard — places content on your system clipboard.
  • Upload to S3 — sends the current image to the exact presigned URL you paste, directly to the bucket you control; nowhere else.
  • Screen/desktop capture — uses Chrome's own "choose what to share" picker; the extension only receives what you choose to share.

No other network requests are made.

Permissions and why

  • activeTab, tabs, host access — read the current tab's URL/title and capture the visible page.
  • tabCapture, desktopCapture, offscreen — capture/record the screen or a window (via the browser's picker).
  • scripting — the in-page selection overlay and full-page stitching.
  • storage — hold a capture briefly and store preferences on your device.
  • downloads — save exported files.
  • clipboardWrite — copy images/text/links when you choose "Copy".
  • commands — keyboard shortcuts.

Children's privacy

The extension is a general-purpose tool, not directed at children, and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes
